Introduction

TeachSheet AI is an educational SaaS platform that helps teachers generate worksheets, classroom activities, and printable educational materials. This Privacy Policy explains how TeachSheet AI collects, uses, stores, and protects information when teachers use the platform.

The purpose of this policy is to provide clear information about account data, authentication, worksheet content, browser storage, and related privacy practices so teachers can use the service with confidence.

Information We Collect

TeachSheet AI may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: information used to create or manage a user account.
  • Email address: the email address used for sign-in, account recovery, or communication.
  • Authentication data: sign-in details managed through Firebase Authentication, including Google Sign-In where selected.
  • Usage information: basic interaction data such as saved worksheets, preferences, or settings used to improve the experience.
  • User-generated worksheet content: prompts, worksheet titles, classroom information, generated content, and project data created by teachers inside the platform.

Firebase Authentication

TeachSheet AI uses Firebase Authentication for secure account management and login. This includes Email/Password sign-in and Google Sign-In when available.

Firebase Authentication is used to support account access, session management, and protection of user accounts. Authentication-related processing is subject to the policies and security practices of Firebase and Google where applicable.

Cookies and Local Storage

TeachSheet AI may use browser technologies such as cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage to improve usability and maintain a smoother teacher workflow.

  • Cookies: may be used for session handling, security, and authentication-related functionality.
  • Local Storage: may be used to remember user preferences, worksheet settings, school settings, and onboarding state.
  • Session Storage: may be used for temporary browser-session behavior where appropriate.
  • User preferences: language choices, UI state, and other classroom workflow preferences may be stored locally in the browser.

These tools are used to improve convenience, reduce repetitive input, and support faster worksheet creation.

How We Use Information

TeachSheet AI may use information for the following purposes:

  • Account management: creating, authenticating, and maintaining teacher accounts.
  • Service improvement: understanding how the platform is used so the worksheet builder can be improved over time.
  • User support: responding to questions, troubleshooting issues, or helping with account or worksheet-related requests.
  • Security and fraud prevention: protecting the platform, user accounts, and service integrity.

Data Protection

TeachSheet AI uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user data. These measures may include secure authentication tools, controlled access to account functionality, and reliance on trusted infrastructure providers.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but TeachSheet AI aims to apply practical safeguards appropriate for an educational SaaS platform used by teachers.

User Rights

Users may have the ability to:

  • Access their data: review the information associated with their use of the service.
  • Update their information: edit classroom details, saved settings, and other account-related data where available.
  • Request account deletion: contact the platform owner to request deletion of their account or related information.
  • Contact the platform owner: ask questions about privacy or data handling practices.

Third-Party Services

TeachSheet AI may rely on third-party services to operate the platform. These may include:

  • Firebase: for authentication, account handling, and related infrastructure services.
  • Google Authentication: for Google Sign-In and identity-based account access where enabled.
  • Hosting providers: infrastructure or deployment services used to deliver the website and application.

These providers may process information as part of the technical operation of the service.
