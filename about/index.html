Product Overview

TeachSheet AI helps teachers move from idea to printable worksheet faster. It combines prompt-based generation, structured classroom settings, bilingual support, printable previews, and export-ready layouts so educators can spend less time formatting and more time preparing lessons.

Worksheet generation

Create classroom materials from prompts, presets, and structured teacher inputs.

Printable workflow

Preview the worksheet in A4 format before exporting a print-ready PDF.

Bilingual support

Use the product in English and French for real school contexts.

Mission

The mission of TeachSheet AI is simple: save teachers time and improve classroom preparation. The product is designed to reduce repetitive formatting work and help teachers prepare cleaner, more usable educational materials faster.

Product Features

Worksheet generation

Create structured activities using prompts, examples, presets, and teacher-friendly defaults.

Ready-to-use templates

Choose from classroom-friendly worksheet styles without building layouts manually.

Printable PDF export

Generate export-ready A4 worksheets for classroom printing and correction workflows.

Classroom-friendly design

Keep worksheets readable, printable, and visually balanced for real teaching use.

Teacher-focused workflow

Use presets, examples, and fast defaults to reduce friction and speed up worksheet creation.

Trust

Built by a teacher for teachers.

TeachSheet AI is not positioned as a generic content tool. It is shaped around real classroom preparation, real printable output, and the everyday needs of primary teachers.

Future Vision

TeachSheet AI is evolving toward a larger SaaS platform for educational productivity. The long-term vision is to support teachers with practical, classroom-ready digital tools that save time, improve preparation quality, and support modern educational workflows.
