General Questions

What is TeachSheet AI?

TeachSheet AI is an educational SaaS platform that helps teachers create worksheets, classroom activities, and printable educational materials faster.

Is TeachSheet AI free?

TeachSheet AI may offer free access to core features, and additional plans or premium options may be introduced in the future as the platform grows.

Can teachers export worksheets as PDF?

Yes. TeachSheet AI is built around printable A4 workflow, and teachers can export worksheets as PDF for classroom printing and correction.

Who can use TeachSheet AI?

The platform is designed primarily for teachers, especially primary school teachers, but it can also be useful for tutors, school support staff, and educational content creators.

Does TeachSheet AI replace teachers?

No. TeachSheet AI is a support tool. It helps teachers prepare materials faster, but teachers still review, adapt, and guide the final classroom experience.

Is the tool suitable for primary school?

Yes. TeachSheet AI is designed with primary classroom workflow, printable readability, and teacher-first pacing in mind.

Can it be used in classrooms?

Yes. The platform is intended for real classroom use, including practice sheets, remediation activities, assessment pages, and printable homework.

Privacy and Data

Does the website collect student data?

TeachSheet AI is designed for teacher workflow and worksheet creation. It does not position itself as a student tracking platform. Classroom text entered by teachers may be used to generate worksheet output, while account-related handling follows the Privacy Policy.

Does TeachSheet AI use browser storage?

Yes. The website may use local storage, session storage, and related browser technologies to save preferences and improve the user experience.

How does login work?

TeachSheet AI uses Firebase Authentication for secure account access, including Email/Password login and Google Sign-In where enabled.
